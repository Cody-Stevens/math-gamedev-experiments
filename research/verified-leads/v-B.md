### G3.1 — STRETCH

- Pitch: A generator can give every pairing equal local odds while producing only three whole-map outcomes.
- Paper check: Accurate. `Entropy-and-Face-Dimension-of-the-Perfect-Matching-Polytope-September-23-2026/build/sections/10-examples.tex`, Proposition `ex:prop-klee-face` and equation `ex:eq-klee-values`, explicitly establish the unique uniform prescribed-marginal law, `H=ln(3)`, `F=m ln(3)`, and `B=2m ln(3/2)`. The argument excludes matchings crossing an inserted triangle cut three times; it does not claim only three matchings exist. Independent enumeration confirms five matchings and maximum marginal error `4/15` for the eight-vertex example.
- Bridge / novelty: The diagnostic is coherent, but the construction and balanced-law restriction are classical ingredients. The section identifies Klee graphs, earlier three-matching examples, and prior balanced matching laws. Elementary odd-cut expectations already force the three outcomes. The paper's additional interpretation is the entropy obstruction, not a new procedural generator. Uniform sampling of all five matchings violates the constraints, so it is a diagnostic baseline, not a competing fair sampler.
- Demo: med — Implement the triangle-expansion rule, inherited matchings, and explicit eight-vertex list from “An eight-vertex obstruction to coefficient one.” Compare exact `H=ln(3)` against the invalid estimate `F-B=4 ln(4/3)` and the paper's valid coefficient-two comparison. Use exhaustive enumeration with an exact marginal-feasibility calculation as the fair baseline; it recovers the same law. A few hundred lines suffice. Measure marginal error, entropy, and support size; do not attribute sampling speed to the new theorem.

### G3.2 — SOLID

- Pitch: Morph symmetric convex rooms with multiplicative support bounds and a guaranteed volume floor.
- Paper check: Accurate. `The-logarithmic-Brunn-Minkowski-conjecture-September-23-2026/build/introduction.tex`, Theorem `thm:main`, equation `eq:main`, defines the Wulff intersection and proves its volume is at least `|K|^(1-lambda)|L|^lambda` for full-dimensional origin-symmetric convex bodies. The geometric mean need not be a support function. A bounded intersection using a subset of directions contains the continuum body, so the floor survives exact finite half-space approximation. Including all endpoint facet normals makes the polyhedral endpoints exact.
- Bridge / novelty: Coherent, and the new guarantee matters for generic symmetric 3D bodies without the earlier common symmetries. Support interpolation and clipping are established techniques. Classical arithmetic support interpolation, the Minkowski sum, already supplies a volume floor by Brunn–Minkowski, but generally gives a larger body: the logarithmic Wulff body is contained in it. The useful distinction is the smaller multiplicative morph with its own guarantee. Matched-vertex interpolation alone is a weak baseline because its correspondences can manufacture collapse.
- Demo: high — Implement support maxima and paired half-space clipping from the opening definitions and `eq:main`, using generic centrally symmetric 3D polyhedra. Compare with classical Minkowski interpolation using the same directions and numerical tolerances; retain vertex interpolation as an optional third view. Measure volume/floor, logarithmic-versus-Minkowski volume, and update time. A few hundred lines suffice with a modest plane count. Recompute support values from the original endpoints when refining directions, and report clipping tolerance separately from the mathematical bound.

### G3.3 — STRETCH

A small simulator is feasible, but include nonzero self-images, periodic copies, and a cutoff-error check. Frame this as exploration of broader certification, not a new speedup.
